Using them in Chamber

Import

  1. Download a library from its site and unzip it.
  2. In Chamber, click Convolution, then Browse... and Import..., and pick the library's folder.
  3. Chamber copies it into its IR Library, groups the files into rooms and finds any photos.

Surround and 3D

Chamber maps every IR channel to your track's speakers, from mono to 7.1.4. Front, rear and height files of one room become one surround room, and B-format files are decoded to your speakers. If a library names its files its own way, use Build room... to set it up once.

Licences

Each library's licence is set by its publisher. Some ask for credit (for example "University of Huddersfield, CC-BY 4.0"). Read the terms on its site before using it in your work.
